# fairdm-geo

A FairDM add-on that gives a research data portal ready-made geoscience models: rock samples,
sampling locations and boreholes, described with terms from published controlled vocabularies.

- [How-to guides](how-to/index.md) take you through one task.
- [Reference](reference/index.md) describes the models and the vocabularies they use.
- [Explanation](explanation/index.md) covers why the package works the way it does.

The [contributing standards](contributing/index.md) are for the package's maintainers.
